Common Questions

FAQ

What is a mobile notary?

A mobile notary travels to the client’s location to perform notarial acts. Instead of visiting a fixed office, the notary meets you at home, work, a hotel, or another agreed place to notarize documents.

Where can you meet me?

We meet clients throughout Fontana, San Bernardino, and Greater Los Angeles at homes, offices, hotels, hospitals, businesses, closing locations, and other agreed-upon sites.

Do I need to provide identification?

Yes. Valid government-issued photo identification is required for notarial acts, consistent with California law. Acceptable forms typically include a current driver’s license, passport, or other government-issued ID that meets statutory requirements.

What types of documents can you notarize?

We perform standard California notarial acts such as acknowledgments and jurats on a wide range of documents. We cannot provide legal advice regarding the content or suitability of any document. If you are uncertain whether a document requires notarization, consult an attorney.

How much does mobile notary service cost?

Mobile Notary pricing is Standard $75, Executive $125, and Premier $200, plus applicable statutory California notarial fees. Exact fees are confirmed when you request service.

Are statutory notarial fees included?

Statutory California notarial fees are separate from the mobile service fee and are charged as applicable under state law.

Do you provide loan signing services?

Yes. Loan Signing Services are currently available. Pricing is Standard $175, Complex $225, and Specialty $250+. These services support real estate, lending, escrow, title, attorneys, businesses, and private clients.

Do you offer same-day service?

Same-day availability depends on schedule and location. Submit a request with your preferred timing and we will respond regarding availability. We do not guarantee same-day service.

Do you serve outside Fontana?

Yes. Primary focus is Fontana and San Bernardino, with service extending throughout Greater Los Angeles. Contact us with your location to confirm coverage.

Are Process Serving and Live Scan available yet?

No. Process Serving is Coming Soon. Live Scan / Biometric ID is Coming Soon — Phase II. These services are not currently bookable. Mobile Notary and Loan Signing Services are available now.

Is travel included in the service fee?

Yes. Travel within a 10-mile radius of Fontana is included in the service fee. Locations beyond this radius are often accommodated and any additional travel consideration is confirmed when you request service.
