Common Questions

FAQ

What is a mobile notary?

A mobile notary travels to the client’s location to perform notarial acts. Instead of visiting a fixed office, the notary meets you at home, work, a hotel, or another agreed place.

Do I need to provide identification?

Yes. Valid government-issued photo identification is required for notarial acts, consistent with California law.

Are statutory notarial fees included?

No. Statutory California notarial fees ($15 per signature under Government Code § 8211) are separate from the mobile service fee and are charged as required by law.

Do you offer same-day service?

Same-day availability depends on schedule and location. Submit a request with your preferred timing and we will respond regarding availability.
